# Preregistration: Saturation Law Formalization (W3_26)

## 1. Theoretical Motivation
In standard $\Lambda$CDM, a dark energy equation of state $w < -1$ leads to a "Phantom Big Rip" singularity. In this scenario, the scale factor $a \to \infty$ in finite metric time, while the dark energy density $\rho_{DE} \to \infty$ and pressure $P_{DE} \to -\infty$. This results in infinite tidal forces that violently tear apart all bound structures, from galaxy clusters down to atomic nuclei.

RefG naturally produces an effective $w_{\rm eff} < -1$ due to the relaxation of the vacuum pressure. However, the ontological interpretation of this mathematical asymptote is radically different. In RefG, the density of the vacuum is not an independent fluid but a geometric property tied to the base environment's pressure $P$. We must formalize what physically happens as $a \to \infty$ to replace the "Big Rip" with the "Vacuum Saturation Law".

## 2. Methodology
We will calculate the asymptotic behavior of three fundamental physical quantities as the scale factor $A \to \infty$:
1. **Background Pressure ($P$)**: According to the RefG metric mapping, $P = P_{\rm ref} A^{-6/7}$.
2. **Effective Gravitational Mass ($M_{\rm eff}$)**: The mass of oscillon structures scales directly with pressure: $M_{\rm eff} \propto P \propto A^{-6/7}$.
3. **Internal Clock Rate ($d\tau/dt$)**: The rate of internal physical processes relative to metric time is $d\tau/dt = \Omega = A^{-3/7}$.

We will plot these three normalized quantities as a function of remaining metric time $t_{\rm remaining}$ up to the final finite time $t_{\rm end} \approx 46.8$ Gyr.

## 3. Expected Outcomes
- As $A \to \infty$, we expect $P \to 0$, $M_{\rm eff} \to 0$, and $d\tau/dt \to 0$.
- **Physical Interpretation**: Rather than a violent "rip", the universe experiences a gentle fading. As the background pressure drops to zero, oscillon structures lose their effective mass and dissipate back into the homogeneous base environment. The internal clocks slow down and eventually stop ($d\tau/dt = 0$). 
- This represents an asymptotic approach to absolute thermodynamic equilibrium (Vacuum Saturation), completely removing the unphysical infinite-energy singularity of the standard Big Rip.
