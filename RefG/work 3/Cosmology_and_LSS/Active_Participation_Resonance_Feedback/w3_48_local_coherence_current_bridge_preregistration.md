## Frozen local audit

The minimal conditional energy ledger is

    epsilon_L' + div F_L = -t_LN - t_LR
    epsilon_N' + div F_N =  t_LN - t_NR
    epsilon_R' + div F_R =  t_LR + t_NR.

The three internal sources sum to zero, so

    epsilon_total' + div F_total = 0.

This is the local form of the W3-39/W3-47 energy bookkeeping. The stationary
rule selects zero cycle-averaged outward radiative flux for the source-locked
trace.

For the candidate participation charge,

    Q_C/Q_C0 = eta a^3,
    Q_C' = S_C - F_C,
    eta'/eta = -3 H_a + Gamma_C.

The frozen scale dictionary gives

    H_A = H_a - (1/2) eta'/eta.

Solving these two rate equations gives

    eta'/eta = -(6/5) H_A + (2/5) Gamma_C,
    H_a = (2/5) H_A + (1/5) Gamma_C.

Therefore

    R_eta = eta'/eta + (6/5) H_A = (2/5) Gamma_C.

The W3-47 homogeneous law and zero net candidate coherent-charge
production/leakage are exactly equivalent within the selected
Q_C/Q_C0=eta a^3 identification and frozen dictionary.

stationary trace, free radiation, and the conditional energy bins. They do
not construct n_C[Phi_F], j_C[Phi_F], or a symmetry/topological invariant

determine Gamma_C. They derive the W3-47 homogeneous law precisely when that
result is Gamma_C=0. The stage ends at the construction of this local current
and map.
