## Minimum candidate structure

Let X_slow denote the remaining slowly varying foundation state. The
candidate action class is

    Lbar_F = Lbar_F(X_slow, partial_mu theta_C),

with invariance under a constant collective phase shift

    theta_C -> theta_C + constant.

The corresponding formal action current is

    C_F^mu = partial Lbar_F / partial(partial_mu theta_C).

Variation with respect to theta_C gives a source-free local continuity law
only when the phase shift is an exact symmetry of the realized action.
Phase averaging or slow modulation instead produces an adiabatic current
with a remainder that must be retained.

The candidate becomes relevant to RefG cosmology only after the same
foundation realization independently gives a positive coarse-grained map

    coarse_grain_D(C_F^0, X_slow) / reference = eta_F.

This equality is an acceptance test, not a definition of eta_F. The spatial
part of C_F may then be compared with J_R; their equality is also a test.
