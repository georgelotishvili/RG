The internal phase `theta_C` is distinct from process time, the
electromagnetic phase, and particle winding or linking. W3-50 selects that
this sector's interactions depend only on phase differences. Under this
selected rule, a common shift of all participating phases leaves the
post-Genesis foundation-sector action unchanged:

    theta_C -> theta_C + alpha.

The minimum local canonical action is

    S_C = integral d tau d mu_a
          [n_C D_tau theta_C
           - H_C(n_C, D_i theta_C, X_slow)].

`d mu_a` is the conditionally selected post-Genesis measure on the connected
branch, and `X_slow` denotes the remaining phase-shift-invariant state.
`D_tau=partial_tau` in the selected comoving chart, and `H_C` is a scalar per
`d mu_a`, rather than a densitized Hamiltonian. The Hamiltonian density is
left uninstantiated because its detailed form cannot change the current
decision inside the declared symmetry class. Pairwise locking terms may
exchange phase action locally when they depend on phase differences; the
diagonal common shift remains exact.

Variation with respect to `theta_C` gives

    partial_tau(sqrt(h_a) n_C)
      + partial_i(sqrt(h_a) j_C^i) = 0,

with

    j_C^i = -partial H_C/partial(D_i theta_C).

The density is positive by the modulus-square representation. The local
continuity law is exact because it follows from an exact internal phase
symmetry rather than phase averaging.

On the frozen homogeneous and isotropic branch,

    j_C^i = 0.

Here `H_C` is differentiable and rotationally regular at
`D_i theta_C=0`, and `X_slow` contains no surviving background vector.
Isotropy then forces the displayed current to vanish. For a boundary with
coordinate velocity `v_b^i`, the outward phase-action flux is formed from
`j_C^i-n_C v_b^i`. The selected boundary is fixed in the comoving chart, so
`v_b^i=0` and the relative flux vanishes independently of the stationary
radiative-energy rule.

measure branch, also used by W3-47,

    d mu_a/d mu_a0 = a^3.

the relative boundary flux. In the W3-48 notation,
