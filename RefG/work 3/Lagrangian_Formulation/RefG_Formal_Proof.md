## 3. Lovelock's Theorem and the Einstein Tensor ($G_{\mu\nu}$)
Once 4D covariance is established as the effective symmetry of the physical space (Postulate 1), the mathematical structure of the macroscopic theory is rigidly constrained.

By Lovelock's theorem, the only symmetric, divergence-free rank-2 tensor that can be constructed from the metric (and its first and second derivatives) in a 4D covariant theory is the Einstein tensor $G_{\mu\nu}$ (up to a cosmological constant). 
Because the physical defect density must be conserved ($\partial_\mu \theta^{\mu\nu} = 0$), the geometric tensor describing this density *must* be the Einstein tensor. Thus, the effective field equations are exactly the linearized Einstein Field Equations:
$$ G_{\mu\nu} = \kappa T_{\mu\nu} $$
where $T_{\mu\nu}$ is the effective stress-energy tensor of the defects.

## 4. Deser's Theorem and the Full Non-Linear EFE
To elevate the linear Fierz-Pauli action (Postulate 2) to a complete theory of gravity, we apply standard physical self-interaction.

This infinite series exactly and uniquely sums to the full non-linear Einstein-Hilbert action:
$$ S_{EH} = \int d^4x \sqrt{-g} R $$
