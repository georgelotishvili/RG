## 1. Null transport and moving clocks

Begin with the covariant null Hamiltonian H4=g^{ab}k_a k_b/2=0. Derive, for s=+1 and -1 separately,

    f_s=sigma*(s-zeta), H_T=-k_T=f_s*k_r,
    dr/dT=f_s,
    d ln E/dT=-partial_r f_s
             =sigma*partial_r zeta-(s-zeta)*partial_r sigma.

Recompute the metric Christoffels and check the affine geodesic equation with dT/dlambda=E/sigma. No coordinate-energy conservation is imposed in a time-dependent metric.

For each moving observer derive

    w=sigma*(v-zeta), q=sigma/gamma,
    omega=gamma*(1-s*v)*E.

Let J be the fixed-time radial flow Jacobian along a connecting ray, initially one, with dJ/dT=(partial_r f_s)J. A variable launch time changes the initial position as well as the ray's integration interval. Hence derive

    dT_o/dT_e=[(w_e-f_e)/(w_o-f_o)] J,
    d tau_o/d tau_e=(q_o/q_e)(dT_o/dT_e),
    (d tau_o/d tau_e)(omega_o/omega_e)=1.

The denominator is a transverse intersection condition. For timelike radial observers and finite positive sigma it cannot vanish through v=s. These relations compare neighboring signals belonging to the same protocol; they do not identify a path-independent scalar potential.

## 2. Local ruler and finite radar protocol

Boost the local coframe theta0=sigma dT, theta1=dr+sigma*zeta*dT into the observer rest frame. On local simultaneity d tau_u=0 derive

    dT=gamma*v*d ell/sigma,
    dr=gamma*(1-zeta*v)*d ell,
    ds^2=d ell^2.

For reflection at a mirror of radial speed v_m, with incident sign s and outgoing sign -s, local elastic frequency equality gives

    E_after/E_before=(1-s*v_m)/(1+s*v_m).

Use the observer's own elapsed proper time on an actual send/reflect/return path to define radar time and distance. For the fixed observer and fixed mirror separated by L in Minkowski space, the production protocol must return elapsed time 2L and radar distance L.

- PG route: integrate (r,ln E) against T with background inversion and endpoint events r-R(T,a_target)=0. Use event direction +1 outward and -1 inward. Check both actual arrivals and the reflection frequency.
