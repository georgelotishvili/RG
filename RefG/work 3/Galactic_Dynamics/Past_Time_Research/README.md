 

# Past-Time Research Ledger

The active branch consists of W3-17 and W3-18.

W3-17 is the PHANGS input-integrity registry. It pins the registered raw
catalogue, rotation-curve, pattern-speed, arm-mask, environment-mask, and
documentation products used by the pilot.

W3-18 is a preregistered relative stellar-clock diagnostic for NGC 4303.
Its saved result is honestly OPEN:

- 179 selected clusters: 76 anchors and 103 clock objects;
- the diagnostic central fit is q=0.56667;
- q=1 lies inside the 68% profile interval [0.08, 1.06];
- Delta BIC(M0-M1)=-4.45239, favoring the common-ticking benchmark;
- zero of four radial blocks satisfy the joint sign-and-leverage gate;
- full angular HST selection coverage is not established;
- permutation, bootstrap, injection, external-pattern-speed, and robustness
  channels were not run.

This pilot supplies no evidence for q>1, but its failed preconditions also
prevent a powered negative or RefG-falsifying conclusion. It does not infer
an absolute historical clock rate.

The earlier color/age-proxy helper and duplicate observational inputs were
removed because they had no surviving consumer. Only W3-17-registered
PHANGS raw products remain in the local PHANGS input directory.
